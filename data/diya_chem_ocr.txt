### Question 07.5
**Calculate the mass of carbon dioxide produced when 6g of propane is fully combusted.**
- The balanced chemical equation for the combustion of propane ($C_{3}H_{8}$) is $C_{3}H_{8}+5O_{2}\rightarrow3CO_{2} + 4H_{2}O$.
- The molar mass of propane ($C_{3}H_{8}$) is $(3\times12)+(8\times1)=44g/mol$.
- The molar mass of carbon - dioxide ($CO_{2}$) is $12+(2\times16)=44g/mol$.
- From the equation, 1 mole of propane ($44g$) produces 3 moles of carbon dioxide ($3\times44 = 132g$).
- If 44g of propane produces 132g of carbon dioxide, then 6g of propane will produce $\frac{132}{44}\times6 = 18g$.
- **Mass = 18 g** (Handwritten answer indicating the calculated mass of carbon dioxide)
