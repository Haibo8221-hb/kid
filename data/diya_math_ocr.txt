8 According to Einstein’s theory of relativity, the mass of a particle is given by the formula $m = \frac{m_0}{\sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{v}{c}\right)^2}}$ where $m_0$ is the mass of the particle at rest, $v$ is the speed of the particle, and $c$ is the speed of light. 
a. Make $v$ the subject of the formula. 
Handwritten solution:
$ \frac{m_0}{m} = \sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{v}{c}\right)^2} $
$ \left(\frac{m_0}{m}\right)^2 = 1 - \left(\frac{v}{c}\right)^2 $
$ \left(\frac{m_0}{m}\right)^2 - 1 = -\left(\frac{v}{c}\right)^2 $
$ 1 - \left(\frac{m_0}{m}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{v}{c}\right)^2 $
$ \frac{v}{c} = \sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{m_0}{m}\right)^2} $
$ v = c \cdot \sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{m_0}{m}\right)^2} $
b. Find the speed necessary to increase the mass of a particle to three times its rest mass, i.e., so that $m = 3m_0$. Give the value for $v$ as a fraction of $c$.
Handwritten solution:
$ v = c \left( -\frac{m_0}{3m_0} + 1 \right) $ (This appears to be incorrect.)
Correct Approach:
 Substitute $m = 3m_0$ into the formula rearranged in part a:
$ 3m_0 = \frac{m_0}{\sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{v}{c}\right)^2}} $
$ 3 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{v}{c}\right)^2}} $
$ \sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{v}{c}\right)^2} = \frac{1}{3} $
 Square both sides:
$ 1 - \left(\frac{v}{c}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{9} $
$ \left(\frac{v}{c}\right)^2 = 1 - \frac{1}{9} = \frac{8}{9} $
$ \frac{v}{c} = \sqrt{\frac{8}{9}} = \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3} $
c. A cyclotron increased the mass of an electron to $30m_0$. At what speed was the electron travelling, given that $c \approx 3 \times 10^8 $ m/s?
Handwritten solution:
$ v = 3 \times 10^8 \left( \frac{m_0}{30m_0} + 1 \right) $ (This is incorrect.)
Correct Approach:
Substitute $ m = 30m_0 $ into the formula rearranged in part a:
$ 30m_0 = \frac{m_0}{\sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{v}{c}\right)^2}} $
$ 30 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{v}{c}\right)^2}} $
$ \sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{v}{c}\right)^2} = \frac{1}{30} $
 Square both sides:
$ 1 - \left(\frac{v}{c}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{900} $
$ \left(\frac{v}{c}\right)^2 = 1 - \frac{1}{900} = \frac{899}{900} $
$ \frac{v}{c} = \sqrt{\frac{899}{900}} \approx 0.99944 $
$ v \approx 0.99944 \times 3 \times 10^8 \approx 2.99832 \times 10^8 \ \text{m/s} $
