## Q2 范文：语言分析题（8/8 满分）

**题目**：How does the writer use language to describe the hyena?

**文本来源**：Life of Pi（Martel）

### 学生范文原文

> The writer describes the hyena's appearance as hideous and undesirable. To begin with Martel describes the hyena as "not well served by its appearance" suggesting it has an unfortunate appearance which does not suit it. A reader may pity the creature for this matter. The author then uses the hyperbole "ugly beyond redemption" to suggest that not even a heaven-sent miracle could fix the poor creatures' looks as they are so ugly.
>
> Martel also uses metaphor to describe the spotted hyena's coat as "a bungled mix of colours" as though it doesn't seem to have all of the same fur and has instead borrowed the colours of other creatures in a mangy manner. Furthermore, by comparing the hyena's spots to those of a leopard's by saying the hyena has "none of the classy ostentation of a leopard's" it implies that despite similarities with other species a hyena will never be mistaken for another. If you look more closely, the word "classy" used not to describe the hyena suggests it has a wild and inelegant nature in comparison to more agile creatures like leopards.
>
> Martel mentions the hyena's spots in an even more negative way when he uses the simile, "like the symptoms of a skin disease." This insulting description depicts the hyena to appear permanently diseased and unkempt.
>
> Finally, Martel describes the hyena's body parts as individually dog-like however when put together they are "like no dog anyone would want as a pet." This final description finalises the disgust felt by those who look at a hyena and how severely revolting and un-loveable the species is.

### 考官评语

| 维度 | 评价 |
|------|------|
| **What Went Well** | 有效识别多种语言技巧（hyperbole, metaphor, simile）；每个引用都有解释；分析逻辑从外貌递进到情感反应 |
| **Even Better If** | 解释要更深——不只说"让人觉得恶心"，还要说"反映了什么更大的主题"；多用术语如 connotation、tone |
| **Stronger Writing** | 示范改写："The hyperbole 'ugly beyond redemption' not only emphasizes the hyena's grotesque appearance **but also evokes a profound sense of pity and revulsion in the reader**, suggesting the creature is beyond the hope of beauty or acceptance."（注意：解释了两层效果，不只是字面意思） |
| **Mark** | **8/8** |

### Q2 核心公式（Diya 必须掌握）

```
引用 → 技巧名 → 字面意思 → 对读者的影响 → 与主题的关联
```

每个引用要做到 **"2个解释"（Mr Salles 的 Points Make Prizes 法则）**：
- 解释1：这句话字面在说什么
- 解释2：这给读者什么感觉/反应/联想

## Q3 范文：结构分析题（满分）

**题目**：How has the writer structured the text to interest the reader?

### 学生范文原文

> The extract begins by focusing on the hyena and what Pi's father had told him about them before in excruciating detail such as that they are "hardy attackers" and "clever". This foreshadows danger Pi may or may not face later on and an atmosphere of suspense is created. A reader would worry for Pi due to his unfortunate situation.
>
> The focus then returns to the hyena alone and its unruly appearance which slightly reduces the tension due to the narrator's belittling yet highly insulting description of the beast he seems to fear so much. This can be seen in the simile "like the symptoms of a skin disease" and the phrase "the head is broad and too massive" suggests that alongside his undeniable fear Pi is still criticizing his companion. The enhanced focus seems to decrease the tension as the hyena doesn't do anything and the pace is slowed. The detailed description allows the reader to immerse themselves into the situation thoroughly.
>
> However, this all quickly changes when the hyena "leapt over the zebra" and starts doing laps around the zebra. This unpredictable behaviour enhances tension due to the suddenness of the hyena's movement, and the pace is quickened. The reader feels as though they are stuck on the boat with Pi and the hyena. The shorter sentences help create chaos on the boat. Pi then focuses on his own reaction in which he was "seized by fear", emphasizing the danger he is in.
>
> Towards the end of the extract the tension spikes once more as the hyena stops and "lifted its eyes and rested them on me". Here the extract meets a climax as the viciously described hyena stares at the protagonist. This tension however quickly dissipates as the tone later becomes more relaxed and the hyena continues to go in circles. The first person narrative that had led throughout enhanced the tension for the entire extract making the reader experience what Pi is themselves in vivid detail.
>
> In the final paragraph "Things ended in typical hyena fashion" suggests that Pi doesn't recognise the hyena as a threat temporarily as he can assume it next moves. The tone becomes less cautious and Pi seems pitiful for the anguished creature as it shakes and whines in its own skin. The reader is relieved and significantly calmed due to Pi's temporary safety.

### Q3 核心公式

```
开头聚焦 → 情绪/氛围 → 转折点 → 新焦点 → 节奏变化 → 高潮 → 结尾语气
```

关注：**focus shifts**（焦点怎么转）、**pace**（节奏快慢）、**tone**（语气变化）、**reader's journey**（读者经历了什么情绪旅程）

## Q4 评价题：考官策略（20分）

**核心原则**：Q4 = Q2（语言分析）+ Q3（结构分析）+ 你的评价立场

### 三条铁律

1. **Methods-based approach**：每段必须从"作者用了什么方法"开始，不是从"我觉得"开始
   - ❌ 错误："I agree the father was cruel because he ignored the child"
   - ✅ 正确："The writer uses the adverb 'delicately' to show that Roland respects the letters, **supporting the idea** that they are special"

2. **回应陈述的两面**：题干通常有两个部分（如"funny AND frightening"），两边都要处理。可以同意一边不同意另一边，**微妙立场很加分**。

3. **禁止空泛的社会批判**：不要写"这是父权社会的体现"那种飘的话。粘着文本细节写。

### Q4 段落模板

```
I agree/disagree with the statement that [题干]. 
The writer uses [技巧] in "[引用]" to [效果], 
which supports/challenges the idea because [解释].
Furthermore, the structural choice to [结构手法] 
reinforces this by [结构效果].
```

## 针对 Diya 的核心建议

Diya 的问题不是词汇量，而是 **analysis paragraph 结构不对**。从范文可以提炼出一个她可以直接用的模板：

```
1. 开头句：The writer presents [主题] as [形容词/观点]...
2. 引用 + 技巧名：This is shown through the [metaphor/simile/adverb] "____"
3. 字面解释：suggesting that...
4. 深层效果：This makes the reader feel/think... because...
5. （进阶）联系主题：This links to the wider idea of...
```

**练习方法**：拿一篇 extract，每个引用强迫自己写两层解释。先用这个模板写 5 篇，形成肌肉记忆。
