## Structure First: What 4 A-Levels Actually Means

```
A-level Maths           = Course 1 (2 years, UCAS 56pts)
A-level Further Maths   = Course 2 (2 years, UCAS 56pts)
A-level Physics         = Course 3 (2 years, UCAS 56pts)
A-level Chemistry       = Course 4 (2 years, UCAS 56pts)

Total: 4 separate qualifications. Not "Maths + a bit extra."
```

Diya originally wrote "Maths… Further Maths?" as if FMaths was an add-on to Maths. It's not — it's its own full A-level with its own syllabus, exam papers, and UCAS tariff.
