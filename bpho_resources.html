Competition Pathway

IPC Warm-up

Intermediate Physics Challenge — Year 11
1-hour paper, multiple choice + short answer. Designed to stretch GCSE-level physics thinking. First exposure to Olympiad-style problems.
Target: Gold (top 15%)

SPC Bridge

Senior Physics Challenge — Year 12 (Jan-Mar)
1-hour paper, harder than IPC. Tests AS-level physics application. Great prep for Round 1 pacing and problem style.
Target: Gold

Round 1 Main Event

BPhO Round 1 — Year 12 (November)
2 sections: S1 (20 MC, 40 min) + S2 (4 long-form, 80 min). The one that counts for UCAS. Top ~100 advance to Round 2.
Target: Top Gold / Round 2

External Resources

Isaac Physics

Free problem-solving platform by Cambridge. BPhO prep board, graded questions, instant feedback. The single best training tool.

BPhO Official Site

Competition dates, registration info, full paper archive including pre-2020 papers.

200 Puzzling Physics Problems

The canonical Olympiad training book. Problems from Hungarian competitions — ideal for Round 1 deep practice.
Strategy tip: Don't just do papers — read the mark schemes carefully. BPhO awards marks for method and physical reasoning, not just the final answer. A solution that shows clear physical thinking can score better than a lucky correct answer with no working.
